Hello my creative friends,

July!! Amazing to think half the year has gone already. I’m not sure why it always surprises me, time. While I do like the fast paced life, as I am getting older I like the peaceful moments of calm and reflection, when I can find them. I celebrated my birthday back in June and was very spoilt with some new art supplies and fun adventures.

Here are some updates on projects I have been involved in:

The exhibition at Minor Works Building in the City of Adelaide is coming to a close. Helena and I were very lucky to have 3 months to exhibit our work there, and once the work is down I will be updating my ShopSALA page for some specials. A huge thank you to Helena for being my exhibition buddy, and I hope to work with you more and more into the future. @helena.kookaburra

![Musical Joy](/static/images/musical-joy-helena-kookaburra.png)

I was extremely excited to hear that my art contribution for the Postcard Art Exhibition in The Hague, Netherlands was sold on opening day!! Over 1000 artists worldwide submitted their art to the cause, and there was a special opening in the Netherlands over the June long weekend. Below is the work that sold, and all proceeds go towards Alzheimers research. I am very much looking forward to being involved in 2027 when the exhibition will be held in Glasgow, Scotland. If you are looking to purchase some amazing work, head over to http://www.postcardartexhibit.com @paeartforacause

I am working on a piece for the Community Wall at Creative Print Studios in Henley Beach which will be a fun new adventure for me. The team at Up in Frames have always been very supportive and sell amazing products @upinframes So watch this space for an update on that.

I recently bought some new art products which I am planning on doing a quick review for next month, so stay stuned for that. Also, I have now acquired an overhead camera to film me painting, so I will be researching how to upload to YouTube to share with others.

For now, stay safe and keep creating

Amy x
