1-8-2025

Hello my fellow creatives,

It’s hard to believe it’s August, which means it’s also SALA here in South Australia. SALA stands for South Australian Living Artists festival. Even though I’ve been heavily involved in SALA since 2019, I have decided to take a break this year. My priorities are different to where I was a few years ago, and therefore I decided not to push myself to create something FOR something, rather create for me.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/sala-mono-pos-1024x407-1.png?w=775)

After ‘Exploring the Organic’ exhibition in June, with fellow artist Karen Kammermann, I felt deflated and quite out of my ‘art zone’. While I was incredibly pleased to sell one painting, I was disappointed that others did not ‘make a mark’ like I thought they would.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/exploring-the-organic-campbelltown-arthouse-opening-event_54559831981_o.jpg?w=1024)

Right image: “Floral Foray #2” SOLD – Artwork by @amyhamlynart

But that is the life of an artist. Even though I think my work is amazing, and I hear from others that my work is amazing, it doesn’t always resonate into sales. It’s taken me awhile to unpack all of the pieces from the exhibition. I am now in the process of deciding whether I will put some pieces on ShopSALA, or reduce some prices and try and sell them through my social media.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/amy-hamlyn-structural-template-1.jpg?w=775)

If you haven’t had a chance to look at the SALA festival booklet yet, I recommend seeing the From Life photography exhibition at Gallery 1855, and the Creating Together exhibition at Campbelltown ArtHouse in collaboration with Ripples. I am running a Night Owl workshop at Harpers Field community Centre on Tuesday, 19 August, which is a beginners guide to abstract art with using household tools and resources that can be easily obtained i.e. recycling, reusing.

During the month of July I completed a #30dayartchallenge. I used one of my sketch books, a small 12 cm x 12 cm book to draw some black pen sketches. Each day I did something different, and remembered to complete the full 30 days. It’s a small achievement, but a fulfilling one. It also took me to the end of that particular sketch book, which is nice as I get to start a new one. Maybe you could try a challenge starting today!

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/pexels-photo-820673.jpeg)

Until next time, keep making art

Amy 🙂
