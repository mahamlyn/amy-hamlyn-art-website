{"title": "Studio Notes: Autumn Light","date": "27 September 2026","excerpt": "A reflection on how changing light reshapes the way I paint the landscape."}
---

Autumn has a way of changing the atmosphere of a painting before I even begin. The light is softer, the shadows longer, and the whole colour palette seems to deepen. This time of year often invites me back to familiar landscapes, but with a new intensity.

I have been working with gentler marks and more restrained palettes, allowing the warmth of the season to come through in layers rather than a single bright gesture. The aim is not to copy the landscape exactly, but to capture the feeling of being within it.

As the days shorten, the studio feels quieter and more reflective. That stillness seems to make space for new ideas, and it is often in those quieter moments that the next series begins to take shape.
