1-12-2025

Hello to my fellow creatives,

December 2025! That happened fast; I feel like I am still in January. But with the weather we have at the moment in Adelaide, it feels more like winter in June! So much rain, and my cricket loving family are just wanting to get a game in.

5 years ago I started this blog, and next one in 2026 will bring up my 50th blog. What an achievement! Thanks for sticking around and following me on my art journey.

I’ve had a very busy last few weeks, which culminated in hosting a small art market stall at my Dad’s care home last Friday. Although not a lucrative market, it was nice to spend the morning with my parents.
