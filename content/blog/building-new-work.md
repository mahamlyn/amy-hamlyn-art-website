Every new series begins with a pile of references, sketches, and fragments of unfinished thoughts. For me, the real starting point is not a final image but a question: what mood am I trying to hold?

This season, I have been returning to textures found in weathered surfaces and rough coastal edges. I am interested in how a painting can carry both calm and friction at once. Through layered washes, scratched lines, and a deliberate palette, I am trying to make room for that tension.

There is still a lot to discover in the process, which is the most exciting part. The studio becomes a place of testing, revising, and letting an image become something more unexpected than the first idea.
