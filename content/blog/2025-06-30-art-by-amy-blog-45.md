1-7-2025

Hello fellow artists, creatives, hobbyists and makers,

There has been so much happening that I haven’t had a chance to write a blog update lately, so here it is. Firstly, thank you to the dedicated people I have who read and comment on my blog; I really do appreciate you being here. Secondly, as I finalise my website there will be some changes happening to where you find my blog and I will be sure to keep you updated when the time comes. But for now, I hope you enjoy catching up on my art journey with me.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/exploring-the-organic-dl-invite-.png?w=775)

‘Exploring the Organic’ exhibition has finished and the pieces that sold have found their forever homes. It was a pleasure to share the ArtHouse walls with Karen Kammermann and we had a wonderful time sharing gallery sitting duties as well. The whole exhibition was visited by over 100 people throughout the month of June, and the opening event was a huge success too. The experience of devoting yourself to an exhibition, particularly for a whole month, is very draining but also rewarding. I’ve included some photos of the journey we had below.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/exploring-the-organic-campbelltown-arthouse-opening-event_54559826581_o-1.jpg?w=1024)

Photograph taken by @LukeInADL featuring @amyhamlynart and @karen.baytree

I’ve had a birthday since I last shared a blog, so now I am 45 years old writing my 45th blog post! I’ve been very busy celebrating my birthday with family and friends, and enjoying the gifts I was given, particularly the massager I got to massage all those sore joints and muscles!
