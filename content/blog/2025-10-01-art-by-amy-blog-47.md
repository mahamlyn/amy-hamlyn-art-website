1-10-2025

Hello fellow creatives,

Great to be back blogging again! September is a busy month for us, with my son’s birthday and school holidays starting. Now it is October, we are into the season of cricket again, which means our weekends get busier with games and the weeknights become training. It’s a good thing we all love the game!

I’ve been busy preparing for a few upcoming markets being held locally. The main pieces I’ve been producing and packaging include C6 gift cards with envelopes, A5, A4 and A3 original artwork on 300gsm watercolour paper. I package them in acid free plastic sleeves from @thecelloking to keep them damage free. Let’s hope that people are interested in buying some for Christmas gifts.

Another fun project I have been working on is reusing and recycling brown shopping bags that people have used. I managed to get a large donation from a local who gets her shopping delivered, so she ends up with lots of shopping bags that go in recycling bin. I had watched a few videos on Youtube about how to sew the binding on paper books, then turned that idea into my own books. I cover the front/back covers in material I have left over from my Grandma’s estate. Have a look at the photo below. Would you like one? Get in contact with me.

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/recycled-paper-books.jpg?w=764)

I’ve been interested in journaling lately, as well. I could use these little books as journals, or scrap books or even just writing lists and notes.

There are some big art prizes coming up that I am looking to enter some work in. Here is a piece I am pondering over. The colours are giving off some beach vibes, don’t you think?

![Image](https://artbyamyoz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/abstract-art-amy-hamlyn.jpg?w=764)

The biggest news of all I have saved to last: I was accepted into the 2026 Adelaide Fringe Exhibition “Unmasked” which is going to be at Gallery 1855 in Tea Tree Gully. The submission process was really powerful for me because I was able to reflect more on my brain tumour journey and I really think the pieces I will produce are going to be amazing. Can’t wait to share!

Take care and keep creating, Amy
