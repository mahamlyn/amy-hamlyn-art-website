1-5-2026

Hello my fellow creatives,

I have missed blogging and I am very happy to be back.

As May begins, I simply cannot believe how fast this year is flying by! We have recently had a very lovely school holiday 2 week break (after Easter) and did some home jobs, saw movies, enjoyed time together as a family.

In my health world, I am still recovering from some recent surgery. In my art world, things have been slow. Unfortunately the SALA solo show will not be going ahead. And that is ok. There are so many other opportunities to take advantage of. I try to draw or paint or scribble each day. I’m currently completing a piece for the Campbelltown Rotary Art Show, in which I will also be running a painting demonstration (a yearly occurance for me) in a couple of weeks time.

My work continues to be hung in the Minor Works Building in the City of Adelaide, alongside fellow artist Helena Kookaburra. No sales yet, but there is still time.
